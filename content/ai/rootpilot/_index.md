---
title: "RootPilot"
---

RootPilot is an evidence-driven AI debugging framework intended to combine routing knowledge, expert selection, repository retrieval, reasoning, and human feedback to investigate complex engineering issues. The pages below describe design ideas and experiments; they do not imply that every capability is implemented.

- **Routing RAG** — Routing metadata and repository relevance preparation.
- **Multi-Domain Expert Routing** — Repository and subsystem ranking from issue evidence.
- **Repository RAG** — Scoped retrieval of evidence from the likely investigation area.
- **UefiNexus Invalid-Goto Investigation** — A RootPilot workflow experiment using a host-side UEFI project fixture.
